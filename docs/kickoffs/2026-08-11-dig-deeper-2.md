# Kickoff: dig deeper #2 — S3 peak demand met (Grid-India)

**Branch:** `cc/dig-deeper-2-2026-08-11` · **Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Requested by:** user ("let's move ahead")

## What

Light up **S3 "What's called on"** with its first parsed number: all-India **maximum demand met, 239,978 MW on 01-09-2023** (a FY2023-24 record), from Grid-India's Monthly Report March-2024, section 23 "All Time Highest".

The citizen story this unlocks on the S3 card: *of 442 GW built, the grid was asked for at most ~240 GW at once.*

## The bigger unlock (recorded for future digs)

Grid-India's SPA is backed by an **undocumented but clean JSON API**:

- `POST https://webapi.grid-india.in/api/v1/page {"_source":"grdw"}` → route → `FileType` codes
- `POST …/api/v1/file/get-period {"_source":"grdw","_fileType":"REPORTS_MONTHLY_REPORT"}` → periods **2012-13 … 2026-27**
- `POST …/api/v1/file {"_source":"grdw","_type":"REPORTS_MONTHLY_REPORT","_fileDate":"2023-24","_month":"03"}` → file path on `webcdn.grid-india.in`

Monthly reports contain **daily** national/regional peak-met and energy-met tables — the future monthly-resolution S3/S6 pipeline for 13 fiscal years. This kickoff takes only the annual peak; aggregating 12 monthly PDFs per year is a later dig.

## Honesty notes

- This is peak **met**, not peak **demand** (unmet shortage excluded) — the fine print says so; CEA's peak-demand figure comes later with the S2→S3 gap.
- "All Time Highest" as of 31-03-2024 equals the FY24 peak because the record was set in Sep-2023; the parser asserts the date falls inside the fiscal year.
- Invariant: peak met < installed capacity.

## Out of scope

Energy met annual total (needs 12 monthly PDFs), S2, S5, S6, monthly resolution.
