# Kickoff: dig deeper #5 — S6 delivered (FY24 energy met, composite of 12 monthly reports)

**Branch:** `cc/dig-deeper-5-2026-08-28` · **Date:** 2026-08-28 · **Requested by:** user ("merge and move ahead")

## What

Light up **S6 "What reached the local grid"** as *partial*: all-India **energy met, FY2023-24**, computed by summing the 366 daily national values from the "Energy Met at National Level" tables of all 12 Grid-India monthly reports (Apr-2023 … Mar-2024), fetched via the JSON API discovered in dig #2 and archived in `data/raw/SRC-4/monthly/`.

- **Basis, labeled honestly (P-10):** energy MET at the state periphery — the working proxy for "delivered to distribution". It is not a physical transmission-loss measurement; the S5→S6 gap bundles inter-state transmission losses + net cross-border trade + basis differences, published as a single number until the NLDC applicable-ISTS-loss series decomposes it.
- Grade **B** (composite, proxy basis). Invariants: exactly 12 months; per-month day-count must equal the calendar (leap-year February = 29); per-month totals in a 90–170 BU plausibility band; S5−S6 gap within 0–6% of S5.

## Dead sources recorded this dig

- MoP "Power Sector at a Glance": frozen since **27/06/2023** (every later Wayback snapshot identical; new Next.js site dropped the page). Archived a snapshot in `data/raw/SRC-12/` as evidence.
- CEA monthly Executive Summaries: PSP section is month-only (no FY cumulative); March-2024 edition never published online.
- Grid-India monthly report "Summary" pages are images — text extraction returns page numbers only.
