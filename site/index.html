India Electricity Dashboard

From what gets announced to what gets delivered — every stage, every gap, honestly.

How to read this

Each box is a stage of India's electricity system. The chain runs from capacity that exists on paper (megawatts) to electricity actually generated, delivered, paid for, and received (units of energy). Grey stages are not populated yet — either because the parser is still to be written, or because India does not publish the data. This dashboard shows that difference instead of hiding it.

Stages 0–3 are measured in power (MW) — what could be produced at an instant. Stages 4–8 are measured in energy (GWh) — what actually was produced over the year. They are not interchangeable, and the bridge between them is the single most misunderstood step in the sector.
